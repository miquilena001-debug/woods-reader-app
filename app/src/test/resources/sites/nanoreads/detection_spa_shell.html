About NanoReads

NanoReads — personalized reading & writing app for books

NanoReads is a personalized reading and writing software for books and a growing book marketplace. Discover short, addictive stories tailored to your taste, write and publish your own books with AI, and read anywhere across web, iOS and Android — with a free chapter every 8 hours.

Features: personalized book recommendations · write your own books with AI · a full book marketplace · free reading on web, iOS & Android.
